Demystifying Enterprise AI

Core Terminology & Foundations

Clear, precise definitions bridging consumer hype to enterprise pharmaceutical reality.

Artificial Intelligence (AI) Broad Field

Computational systems capable of performing tasks typically requiring human intelligence, including pattern recognition, reasoning, document synthesis, and statistical inference.

Pharma Context: Distinguishing statistical ML (e.g. molecular solubility predictions) from modern Generative AI (clinical report drafting and literature synthesis).
Large Language Models (LLM) Neural Architecture

Deep transformer models trained on massive corpora to predict subsequent tokens. They possess broad language understanding, world knowledge, and translation/synthesis abilities.

Pharma Context: Understanding GPT-4o, Claude 3.5, and Llama 3 as general reasoning engines rather than static relational databases.
RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Gen) Essential Architecture

A framework that retrieves proprietary private documents from an indexed knowledge base before prompting an LLM to generate answers grounded strictly in retrieved context.

Pharma Context: Querying internal Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), Clinical Study Reports (CSRs), and FDA guidance without hallucination.
Assistants vs Agents vs Workflows Spectrum of Autonomy

Chatbots converse. Assistants summarize & suggest. Agents take multi-step tool actions. Workflows follow deterministic orchestration with AI nodes.

Pharma Context: Why regulated GxP environments favor deterministic AI workflows over unbounded autonomous agents.
Prompt vs Context Engineering Human-AI Interface

Prompt Engineering: Crafting phrasing, roles, and instructions. Context Engineering: Dynamically assembling relevant document chunks, system constraints, metadata, and schemas into the limited context window.

Pharma Context: Injecting relevant Pharmacovigilance criteria and ICH guidelines directly into the prompt payload.
Tokens & Vector Embeddings Mathematical Basis

Tokens: Sub-word chunks (~0.75 words). Embeddings: High-dimensional numerical arrays capturing semantic meaning so similar medical terms sit close together in geometric space.

Pharma Context: Enabling semantic search where a search for "renal impairment" retrieves documents describing "kidney disease" or "decreased eGFR".
Temperature & Hyperparameters Inference Controls

Controls the randomness of next-token selection. Low temperature (0.0 - 0.2) enforces deterministic, analytical adherence. Higher temperature (0.7 - 1.0) introduces creative variability.

Pharma Context: Always set temperature = 0.0 for regulatory data extraction and adverse event classification to guarantee reproducibility.
MCP (Model Context Protocol) Open Standard

An open protocol allowing LLMs and AI assistants to securely connect to external data sources, local databases, and enterprise file servers through standardized, permissioned interfaces.

Pharma Context: Connecting Copilot or internal assistants directly to local ETL databases without exposing credentials or unverified API keys.
Fine-Tuning vs RAG Customization Strategy

Fine-Tuning: Updates internal weights to learn specific syntax/tone (costly, stale). RAG: Grounds base model with live external facts without updating weights (fast, source-traceable).

Pharma Context: 95% of pharma use-cases require RAG (traceable audit trails & immediate updates), not model weight fine-tuning.
Local & Air-Gapped AI Zero Cloud Egress

Running open-weights models (Llama-3, Mistral, BioMistral) directly on local GPU servers (Ollama, vLLM) entirely inside the corporate intranet without outbound internet traffic.

Pharma Context: Analyzing pre-patent molecular structures, drug synthesis pathways, and unblinded clinical trial patient data.
LLMOps & Quality Guardrails Production LifeCycle

The operational discipline of evaluating, deploying, monitoring, and tracing LLM pipelines for latency, token costs, hallucination rates, and drift over time.

Pharma Context: Validation testing and version control required under 21 CFR Part 11 software lifecycle regulations.
Multimodal AI & Audio Cross-Domain

Models processing and cross-correlating text, image, tabular, and audio data simultaneously (e.g. OCR on scanned batch records, Whisper audio transcription of doctor interviews).

Pharma Context: Digitizing historical paper lab logs, pathology slide summaries, and medical advisory board audio transcripts.
GAMP 5 & ALCOA+ Integrity GxP Validation

The gold-standard Computerized System Validation (CSV) framework categorizing software into risk tiers (Category 4 Configured vs Category 5 Custom), paired with ALCOA+ data integrity criteria.

Pharma Context: Proving that prompt logs, model inference seeds, and extracted clinical records are Attributable, Legible, Contemporaneous, Original, and Accurate for FDA/EMA audits.
RAG Evaluation Triad Quality Metrics

A formal triad metric measuring: Context Relevance (did retrieval pull the right SOP?), Groundedness / Faithfulness (is the answer backed 100% by context?), and Answer Relevance (does it satisfy the prompt?).

Pharma Context: Running automated evaluation benchmarks (e.g. RAGAS, TruLens) as qualification evidence before releasing AI search across regulatory portfolios.

02

Technical Mechanics: Embeddings, Vectors, Hyperparameters & MCP

Under the hood: Vector spaces, similarity search, Model Context Protocol, and local models

3.0 Hours Technical Track
  • Embeddings & High-Dimensional Semantic Search

    How mathematical text vectors capture bio-medical semantic relationships. Cosine similarity, Euclidean distance, and hybrid search combining BM25 keyword matching with dense vectors.

  • Vector Databases: Architecture & Indexing

    How enterprise vector stores (Azure AI Search, Qdrant, Chroma, PGVector) index millions of scientific publications, clinical trial records, and patent filings using HNSW algorithms.

  • Hyperparameter Tuning: Temperature, Top_P, Frequency Penalties

    Setting temperature to 0.0 for auditing, regulatory tables, and data extraction vs. 0.6 for brainstorming slide titles. Deep dive into repetition penalties and system prompt grounding.

  • MCP (Model Context Protocol) & Secure Tool Calling

    Anthropic's open standard for connecting AI clients to local files, databases, and enterprise APIs. Building safe read-only MCP bridges into laboratory information systems (LIMS).

  • Local AI Deployment (Ollama, vLLM, GGUF/AWQ Quantization)

    Setting up local, air-gapped inference servers inside the pharma corporate firewall. Memory sizing (VRAM requirements, 4-bit/8-bit quantization) for open weights models like Llama 3 and Mistral.

  • Fine-Tuning Architectures (LoRA, QLoRA, PEFT) vs In-Context Grounding

    When parameter-efficient fine-tuning is justified (specialized chemical nomenclature, proprietary SMILES tokens, structured JSON dialects) vs. when fine-tuning creates regulatory liability (catastrophic forgetting, stale knowledge cutoff, high Computerized System Validation re-audit costs).

Engineering Takeaways & Outcomes
  • Ability to architect scalable vector retrieval pipelines without vendor lock-in.
  • Mastery of local offline model execution for confidential chemical IPs.
  • Understanding the MCP specification to replace custom fragile API wrappers.

System Prompt for Automated ETL Safety Extraction Node ETL System Prompt
Ingesting unstructured patient clinical notes into deterministic, validated JSON records for SQL/data warehouse storage
You are an automated Pharmacovigilance (PV) data extraction node in a high-throughput GxP ETL pipeline.

Your task is to ingest unstructured patient incident reports or physician notes and output strictly validated, parseable JSON conforming to ICH E2A safety guidelines.

INPUT TEXT:
"""
[Insert raw clinician note / EHR excerpt]
"""

EXTRACTION RULES:
1. Extract every reported adverse event into an array under the key "adverse_events".
2. For each event, include:
   - "verbatim_term": Exact symptom phrase quoted directly from the input text.
   - "suspect_drug": Trade name or active ingredient mentioned, or "NOT_SPECIFIED".
   - "severity": Must be strictly one of ["MILD", "MODERATE", "SEVERE", "LIFE_THREATENING"].
   - "causality_assessment": Strictly one of ["UNRELATED", "POSSIBLE", "PROBABLE", "DEFINITE"].
   - "action_taken_with_drug": Strictly one of ["DOSE_NOT_CHANGED", "DOSE_REDUCED", "DRUG_INTERRUPTED", "DRUG_WITHDRAWN", "UNKNOWN"].
   - "regulatory_seriousness": Boolean (true if hospitalization, disability, life-threatening, or death occurred; false otherwise).
   - "confidence_score": Decimal between 0.0 and 1.0 indicating extraction certainty.
3. If no adverse events are detected, return an empty array for "adverse_events".
4. Set "requires_expedited_15_day_review": true if any event meets regulatory expedited criteria.

OUTPUT CONSTRAINT:
Output ONLY raw, valid JSON with zero conversational filler, zero markdown backticks, and zero preamble.
Why it works: Enforces strict JSON field constraints and ICH E2A severity enumerations, allowing downstream Python/SQL ETL pipelines to ingest data without parsing errors.

Risk Mitigation & Compliance

Enterprise Security, Data Boundaries & GxP

Essential guidelines answering: What can be shared? Where does data go? Is data used to train public models? And how do we satisfy regulatory audits?

Data Classification Level Example Pharma Data Assets Public Consumer AI (ChatGPT/Gemini Free) Microsoft Copilot Enterprise (M365) Private / Air-Gapped Local AI (Ollama/vLLM)
Tier 1: Public / Generic Published PubMed papers, FDA guidance documents, public patents, generic meeting summaries. Allowed (No IP) Recommended Supported
Tier 2: Confidential Corporate Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), internal training, non-confidential department memos, general financial reports. STRICTLY PROHIBITED Protected by Tenant Boundary Fully Secured
Tier 3: Clinical & Patient Data (PHI/PII) Patient medical notes, adverse event filings with identifiers, unblinded clinical trial patient logs. STRICTLY PROHIBITED De-identified Only (HIPAA/GDPR) Recommended (Air-Gapped)
Tier 4: Core Intellectual Property Unpublished chemical synthesis pathways, molecular SMILES, upcoming patent claims, trade secrets. STRICTLY PROHIBITED Executive Clearance Required Mandatory (Zero Egress)

Model Training & Retention Truths

In Microsoft Copilot Enterprise and Azure OpenAI with enterprise agreements, customer prompts, completions, and documents are never used to train foundation models. Zero Data Retention (ZDR) agreements ensure data is processed in memory and not logged on third-party servers.

21 CFR Part 11 & GxP Validation

Automated AI systems in GxP cannot act as final unreviewed signatories. Every AI-assisted regulatory document requires an audit trail documenting the prompt version, model version, and an explicit Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) qualified review signature.

Hallucination Defenses & RAG Triad

Implement deterministic safeguards: enforce temperature=0.0, use negative prompting ("If not found in context, state UNKNOWN"), require page-level citations for every quantitative claim, and benchmark retrieval using RAG Triad metrics (Context Relevance, Groundedness, Answer Relevance).

GAMP 5 & ALCOA+ Data Integrity

Classify AI implementations under GAMP 5 risk tiers (Category 4 Configured M365 Copilot vs Category 5 Custom ETL/RAG). Guarantee ALCOA+ principles (Attributable, Legible, Contemporaneous, Original, Accurate) with immutable prompt-response audit logging and qualified CSV sign-offs.
