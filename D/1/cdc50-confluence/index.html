01 Milestone

A milestone worth showing up for

For 49 events, Chennai Data Circle has been the meeting point for analysts, engineers, students and founders building with data. CDC50 — Confluence brings that entire story into one room.

This isn't another tech meetup. It's a celebration of the people, connections and ideas that shaped the journey — with the speakers, partners and familiar faces who built it alongside us.

  • 50th event — our flagship milestone, hosted with 400+ participants
  • Powered by Ford Fibonacci at the Ford Global Technology & Business Center
  • Event partner Codebasics running a dedicated partner speaker session
  • Community partner Indian Data Club bringing India's wider data network in
  • Live demos, a fundamentals-vs-AI-first roundtable and open networking
  • Part of a 50-event run across Chennai's data and AI community
