Random Discovery

One Note, Three Color-Coded Decisions

Press one button to surface an eligible note at uniform random, then categorize it instantly. The note stays open so you can keep reading and editing.

How to Read This View:
  • Open Random Note (R): Surfaces an unreviewed note at uniform random from your workspace and opens it in the main editor.
  • Eligible Candidate Count (1452): Live pool size after excluding Vault notes, audit notes, and already-triaged notes under the current filter.
  • Keep (1 / K): Applies 2-keep, confirming the note is relevant and worth retaining.
  • Delete (2 / D): Applies 2-discard as a flag — notes are never deleted automatically.
  • Review (3 / V): Applies 2-review, flagging the note for deeper research or editing.
Triage Science
The three tags are strictly mutually exclusive — applying one strips any previous status. One note, three decisions, instant feedback: a loop fast enough to become a habit.

Progress & Metrics

Live Workspace Progress at a Glance

Progress cards turn an abstract backlog into a visible finish line. See exactly how many notes are left, what you've kept, and what still needs a closer look.

How to Read Each Metric Card:
  • Workspace Review Progress (0% (7 / 1459)): Completion ratio with a smooth gradient progress bar and an explanatory info tooltip.
  • Total Notes (1459): Full workspace count with remaining unreviewed notes shown underneath. Vault notes excluded.
  • Reviewed (7): Notes already triaged, plus the completed percentage.
  • Keep (4) / To Delete (2) / To Review (1): Per-decision breakdown so you always know the shape of your backlog.
Momentum
