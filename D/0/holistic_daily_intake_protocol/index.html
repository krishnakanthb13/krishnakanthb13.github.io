Fuel & Raw Material

Eat for the Body You Want in a Year

Your somatic tissues are literally reconstructed every few months from ingested proteins, lipids, and micronutrients. Ultra-processed foods (UPFs) are engineered around a sugar-fat-salt "bliss point" that dysregulates dopamine D2 receptors in the nucleus accumbens and overrides natural satiety.

Unlocking Whole-Food Bioavailability

Native grains and millets offer dense micronutrients and anthocyanins (such as C3G in Karuppu Kavuni black rice), but contain phytic acid (IP6) which chelates minerals. Traditional preparation degrades IP6 to unlock absorption:

Soaking
Activates endogenous phytases; boosts Fe/Zn solubility up to 23%.
Germination
Catabolizes tannins; elevates B-vitamins & improves glycemic response.
Fermentation
Microbial phytases & low pH yield peak Ca, Fe, Zn & amino acid uptake.
Silent Macronutrients: Hydration drives aqueous enzymatic breakdown and nutrient transport, while slow-wave sleep triggers growth hormone release to assimilate circulating amino acids into tissue.

The Sensory Interface

Taste Is the Interface, Not the Goal

Evolution designed taste to evaluate nutrient density. Modern food decouples "tastes good" from "is good." Worse, non-nutritive sweeteners (NNS) activate T1R2/T1R3 sweet receptors without delivering calories, causing metabolic mismatch, reward prediction errors, and aberrant insulin signaling.

Attentional & Circadian Architecture

What You Look At Shapes What Your Brain Becomes

Endless short-form video feeds exploit intermittent dopamine rewards, altering dorsolateral prefrontal cortex (DLPFC) activity and eroding sustained focus. Simultaneously, light entering retinal ipRGCs governs your master circadian clock (SCN).

20-20-20 Optical Reset

Near-field screens lock ciliary muscles in contraction and cut blink rates by 25%. Every 20 mins, view an object 20 ft away (optical infinity) for 20 secs to relax accommodation and restore tear film.

Optic Flow & Amygdala

Forward walking generates lateral optic flow across the retina, triggering horizontal eye movements that directly inhibit amygdala fear circuitry and lower systemic cortisol.

Circadian Rule: Morning sunlight (>1,000+ melanopic lux) anchors daytime cortisol and sets a 14–16 hour timer for sleep. Evening screens exceed the 1.5 melanopic lux threshold, suppressing melatonin.

Nervous System Baseline

Silence Is a Neurobiological Nutrient

Sound sets the baseline arousal of your autonomic nervous system. Constant background media keeps you trapped in externally focused networks, preventing the Default Mode Network (DMN) from performing narrative integration and memory consolidation.

Hippocampal Neurogenesis via Silence

Research (Kirste et al.) shows that periods of complete silence stimulate precursor cell proliferation and newborn neuron survival in the hippocampus—an effect white noise fails to replicate.

Acoustic Contagion & Your Inner Voice

Elevated cortisol physically alters vocal pitch and formants. Listening to outrage-driven podcasts or chronically stressed voices triggers autonomic co-regulation, elevating your own stress baseline.
