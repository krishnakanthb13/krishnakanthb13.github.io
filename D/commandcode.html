How Credits Work & Limits

  • Resets monthly: Credit budget refreshes automatically at the start of each billing cycle.
  • Top up automatically: Buy extra credits at raw model cost — extra credits roll over and never expire.
  • Deals apply automatically: Permanent discounts and promo multipliers apply to every request, including extra credits.
  • Beyond plan limits: Requests fall back to extra top-up credits. If empty, paid models pause while 100% free models (`Laguna S 2.1`, `Ling 3.0 Flash`) keep working.

Get Started

  1. Install CLI: npm i -g command-code
  2. Sign in and launch: cmd (macOS/Linux) or cmdc (Windows)
  3. Select or upgrade plan from Studio → Billing or the pricing page
