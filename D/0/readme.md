# D/0 — Knowledge Vault

## Why This Exists

This directory is a living, growing knowledge vault. As knowledge is earned—through research, reflection, and deep study—it gets dropped in here as self-contained topic folders. Each folder is a complete "module" on a single subject, pairing a long-form written report with interactive HTML visualizations so the material can be both read and explored.

The goal is simple: **knowing is cheap, structure is everything.** A fact picked up from a sentence decays; a curated, navigable vault compounds.

## How It's Organized

- Each **subfolder** = one topic, earned and curated.
- `readme.md` — the long-form essay / research report (the written knowledge).
- `index.html` — the primary interactive visualization of that topic.
- `0.html`, `1.html`, ... — alternate or companion interactive variants (light themes, simulators, quizzes, etc.), when present.

## Vault Contents

### 📁 [Fear & Courage](./fear_n_courage/)
**The Architecture of Courage** — a cognitive, rhetorical, and clinical analysis of fear, acceptance, and the punctuation of emotion: how a single comma becomes "a pause and a permission," how affect labeling inhibits the amygdala, and how ACT and DBT dismantle experiential avoidance. Synthesizes literary phenomenology, Adorno's philosophy of punctuation, affective neuroscience (Damasio, Lieberman), and third-wave cognitive-behavioral paradigms.

- 📝 [The Full Report](./fear_n_courage/readme.md) — the long-form essay with 60 cited sources. (`readme.md`)
- ☀️ [Interactive Infographic](./fear_n_courage/index.html) — the complete light-themed interactive page: comma cadence lab, archetype evolution radar, Adorno punctuation instrument, fMRI affect-labeling simulator, ACT hexaflex grid, DBT radical-acceptance calculator, and a practical courage toolkit. (`index.html`)

### 📁 [Knowing vs. Accepting](./knowing_vs_accepting/)
**The Epistemology and Somatics of Surrender** — why intellectual "knowing" routinely fails to change behavior, and why acceptance is the costly, embodied act of translating a fact into the nervous system. Synthesizes cognitive psychology (hot-cold empathy gap, cognitive dissonance, knowing-doing gap), somatic psychotherapy (EFT, Gendlin's Focusing, DBT Radical Acceptance), and contemplative epistemologies (Advaita Vedanta's *Paroksha → Aparoksha*, Buddhism's three stages of wisdom).

- 📝 [The Full Report](./knowing_vs_accepting/readme.md) — the long-form essay with cited sources. (`readme.md`)
- 🌑 [Interactive Infographic](./knowing_vs_accepting/index.html) — the complete dark-themed interactive page: radar profile of Knowing vs. Accepting, time-lag bar chart, dual energy-allocation doughnuts, hot-cold empathy-gap simulator, cognitive energy reclamation calculator, step-through somatic pipeline, filterable cross-disciplinary framework matrix, and a self-diagnostic quiz. (`index.html`)
