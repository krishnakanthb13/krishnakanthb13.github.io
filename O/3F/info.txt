The Sociological Geometry of Desire: An Interdisciplinary Analysis of the "Hot, Fun, Sane" Trilemma and the Pathologization of Female AutonomyThe contemporary digital landscape is replete with folk taxonomies and humor-veiled heuristic devices designed to navigate the complexities of modern romance.
