◆ Core Thesis: Scalar Multiplier, Not Directional Vector

Consistency compounds whatever you feed it—including strategic failure.

Popular success narratives treat consistency as an unconditional virtue and a test of raw willpower. Synthesis across cognitive neuroscience, behavioral economics, and organizational psychology reveals a sharper truth: consistency is a phase-dependent design problem. Optimal performance requires exploring until you discover an asymmetric, positive-sum game, engineering low-friction environments to automate execution, and scheduling deliberate recovery to prevent rigid entrapment.

Section 02 • Mathematics & Physics

Ergodicity Lab: Why Time Averages Govern Compounding

In Ole Peters' ergodicity economics, an ensemble average (the average outcome across millions of parallel players at once) frequently diverges from a time average (the geometric compounding experienced by a single person iterating over time). Explore the multiplicative coin-toss simulation below (+50% on heads, −40% on tails) to see why consistency in a flawed structure guarantees ruin despite a positive expected value.

Multiplicative Coin-Toss Simulation (100% Wager)

Heads: ×1.50 (+50%) • Tails: ×0.60 (−40%) • Starting Capital: $100

Key Takeaway: Across an infinite ensemble, wealth grows by +5.0% per round (0.5×1.5 + 0.5×0.6 = 1.05). Yet for a single consistent individual, the geometric time-average multiplier is (1.5 × 0.6)0.5 ≈ 0.9487 (−5.13% decay per round). Consistency only compounds wealth when bet sizing (Kelly Criterion) prevents absorbing barriers.

The Paradox of Skill

As competitive cohorts become uniformly more skilled and consistent, the variance in skill shrinks—which paradoxically increases the relative role of luck in separating winners from runners-up.

Section 04 • Cognitive Neuroscience

Neurobiology: Corticostriatal Transfer & Action Chunking

Consistency feels effortless once the brain physically transfers control from the energy-hungry Prefrontal Cortex (Model-Based) to the automated Dorsolateral Striatum (Model-Free). Ann Graybiel's research shows continuous neural firing collapses into cue-and-reward "action chunks," while Phillippa Lally's longitudinal study reveals how automaticity grows asymptotically over a median of 66 days.

Section 05 • Behavioral Engineering & Safeguards

Environment Design, Pathologies & Strategic Recovery

Multi-lab replication failures dismantled the "ego depletion" willpower myth: mental fatigue is an opportunity-cost signal, not an empty fuel tank. Consequently, durable consistency relies on environmental channel factors, pre-loaded implementation intentions, double-loop feedback to escape sunk-cost traps, and psychological detachment to prevent allostatic overload.

Why Design Beats Motivation: Empirical Effect Sizes

Leventhal Channel Factors (1965) & Gollwitzer Implementation Intentions (94 Studies)

Evidence Summary: Fear-based persuasion changed attitudes but yielded only 3% vaccination action; adding a campus map and schedule prompt (a channel factor) lifted action to 28%. Similarly, "If-Then" plans produce a d = 0.65 effect size by delegating initiation to environmental cues.
