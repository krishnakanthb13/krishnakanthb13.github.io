📖 Why This Vault Exists

Knowledge picked up from a sentence is cheap and reversible—it stays in the head. This vault exists to convert earned knowledge into a durable, navigable structure: every topic is written out as an essay (the report) and rendered as an interactive page (the experience). Knowing needs evidence; a vault needs surrendering the chaos of unorganized notes into one indexed place.

Conventions: each subfolder is one topic. readme.md is the written report, index.html is the primary visualization, and numbered 0.html, 1.html… files are companion variants. This page links every HTML and Markdown file in every subfolder—when a new topic is earned, add a card above and a section to Readme.
